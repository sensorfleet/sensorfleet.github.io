Mikko joined the SensorFleet team as an Account Manager, bringing a versatile
background in sales across both the B2B and B2C markets. He began his sales
career at Nuvoo (formerly Taitonetti), where he worked for over six years. From
there, his path continued as Business Manager at ALTEN Finland and as Sales &
Marketing Manager at Verkotan, before he moved to SensorFleet.

Based in Oulu, Mikko especially enjoys working with new customer relationships
and thrives on straightforward, trust-based sales, both at home and
internationally. In his free time, his competitive spirit comes out on the
floorball court, the basketball court, and the padel court, and that same drive
shows in the way he works with customers.

Don't hesitate to reach out to Mikko if you'd like to talk about network
monitoring and NDR solutions!
